This page records development directions, not capabilities guaranteed by v1.7.0. Consult the [release notes](/en/docs/overview/release-1.7/) for shipped functionality and [GitHub issues](https://github.com/kubeclipper/kubeclipper/issues) for current discussions.

- OCI-based distribution of installation resources
- Kubernetes workload and monitoring views
- Application and add-on lifecycle management
- Additional managed-cluster deployment modes

No delivery dates are promised here. Validate experimental features separately before production use.
